Laser Therapy for Headache Relief: What to Know

By Editorial Team

Laser therapy for headache relief is studied for migraine pain, but evidence is mixed. Learn how it works, its safety profile, and what to ask a provider.

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Quick Summary

Laser therapy for headache relief applies low-level light energy to the skin over the head, neck, or face to ease pain and tension. It is a non-invasive option that some people with chronic headaches ask their clinicians about.

  • Laser devices sold online or in wellness shops are not always used under medical supervision.
  • Ask who will assess your headaches before starting any laser treatment.
  • Do not stop prescribed headache medications before a consultation without talking to your prescriber first.
  • Low-level laser therapy is compared with preventive medication, acute medication, and lifestyle changes.
  • The evidence for laser therapy for migraines is still developing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can laser therapy help relieve headaches?

Some small studies report reduced headache or migraine frequency or pain after low-level laser therapy, but the research is limited and results vary between studies. Larger, well-designed trials are still needed before laser therapy can be considered a proven treatment. Anyone considering it for headaches should discuss their symptoms, medical history, and current medications with a licensed clinician, who can explain what the current evidence does and does not support for their situation.

Is laser therapy safe for headache treatment?

Low-level laser therapy is generally considered to have a low risk of serious side effects when delivered by trained clinicians using appropriate devices. Reported effects are typically mild and temporary, such as slight warmth or minor discomfort at the treatment site. Safety depends on the device, the settings used, eye protection, and the provider's training, so ask about these details before starting treatment.

What should I ask a provider before trying laser therapy for headaches?

Ask which headache diagnosis they are treating and what evidence supports laser therapy for it. Find out how many sessions are typically recommended, how progress will be measured, and what side effects to expect. Ask whether the device is FDA-cleared and whether laser therapy would be used alongside or instead of your current treatment plan. A good provider will explain alternatives and will not promise a cure.

Is laser therapy an alternative to migraine medication?

Laser therapy should not be treated as a replacement for prescribed migraine medication unless a licensed clinician has evaluated you and recommended that change. Stopping or changing medication without guidance can cause problems. Discuss any interest in non-drug options with your prescriber so that your overall plan stays coordinated and safe.
