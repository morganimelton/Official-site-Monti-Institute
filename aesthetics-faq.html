Laser Skin Rejuvenation

Erbium:YAG Laser

Erbium:YAG (Er:YAG) is an advanced skin resurfacing laser that uses a 2,940-nanometer wavelength to precisely remove controlled layers of damaged skin. It works by targeting water within the skin, allowing for precise resurfacing and renewal. This treatment can improve:

  • Fine lines and wrinkles.
  • Sun damage and age-related skin changes.
  • Acne scars and uneven texture.
  • Enlarged-looking pores.
  • Rough or damaged skin.
  • Overall skin quality and appearance.

Erbium:YAG provides precise superficial or deeper resurfacing depending on the treatment protocol. It can remove damaged skin while stimulating the body’s natural wound-healing and collagen-remodeling processes. It is particularly useful for patients seeking more substantial skin texture improvement.

Discomfort varies depending on the depth and extent of treatment. Topical or other appropriate anesthesia may be used. Patients may experience warmth, stinging, or a sunburn-like sensation.

Downtime depends on treatment intensity. More superficial treatments may involve several days of redness and peeling, while more aggressive resurfacing may require one to two weeks or longer for recovery.

Some patients achieve meaningful improvement after one treatment. Others benefit from a series of treatments depending on their skin condition and goals.

  • Redness and swelling.
  • Peeling and crusting.
  • Temporary sensitivity.
  • Changes in skin pigmentation.
  • Infection, including herpes reactivation.
  • Prolonged redness.
  • Scarring in uncommon cases.

Patients seeking improvement in wrinkles, sun damage, acne scarring, and uneven skin texture may be candidates. A consultation is necessary to evaluate skin type, healing history, medications, and the appropriate treatment depth.

Erbium Glass Laser

Erbium Glass is a non-ablative fractional laser technology, commonly operating at approximately 1,540–1,550 nanometers. Unlike ablative resurfacing lasers, it delivers controlled thermal energy into the deeper skin without intentionally removing the outer skin surface. This stimulates collagen remodeling and gradual skin renewal.

  • Fine lines and wrinkles.
  • Acne scars.
  • Enlarged pores.
  • Uneven skin texture.
  • Early signs of skin aging.
  • Mild skin laxity.
  • Sun-related skin changes.

Erbium:YAG is generally used for ablative resurfacing, removing controlled amounts of surface skin. Erbium Glass is generally non-ablative and fractional, stimulating collagen beneath the surface while preserving the outer skin. Erbium Glass generally involves less downtime, while Erbium:YAG can provide more substantial resurfacing when a deeper treatment is appropriate.

Most patients describe warmth, prickling, or a series of small snapping sensations. Topical anesthetic may be used depending on treatment intensity.

A series of approximately three to five treatments may be recommended, depending on the device, settings, skin condition, and desired outcome.

Most patients experience temporary redness, warmth, mild swelling, and occasionally a rough or sandpaper-like texture. Downtime is generally shorter than with ablative resurfacing.

Potential side effects include:

  • Redness and swelling.
  • Temporary darkening or lightening of the skin.
  • Burns or blistering.
  • Infection.
  • Scarring, rarely.

Pico Laser

Pico laser technology delivers extremely short pulses of laser energy, measured in picoseconds (trillionths of a second). It uses photoacoustic effects to fragment targeted pigment into smaller particles that the body can gradually clear. Depending on the wavelength and handpiece, it can also be used for selected skin rejuvenation treatments.

  • Unwanted tattoos.
  • Certain forms of sun-related pigmentation.
  • Selected benign pigmented lesions.
  • Uneven skin tone.
  • Selected acne scars and textural concerns.
  • Overall skin rejuvenation, depending on the treatment platform.

Traditional lasers may rely more heavily on thermal energy. Picosecond lasers deliver extremely short pulses that create a photoacoustic effect, allowing targeted pigment fragmentation with less reliance on prolonged heat exposure.

Yes. Certain Pico laser wavelengths can target tattoo pigments and help break them into smaller particles. Multiple sessions are usually necessary. Results depend on tattoo color, depth, ink composition, age, and location. Complete tattoo removal cannot be guaranteed.

Patients often describe the sensation as small rubber-band snaps against the skin. Discomfort varies by treatment area and intensity.

For skin rejuvenation, a series may be recommended. Tattoo removal often requires multiple sessions separated by appropriate healing intervals.

  • Redness and swelling.
  • Temporary pinpoint bleeding or crusting.
  • Pigment changes.
  • Blistering.
  • Infection.
  • Scarring, rarely.

IPL Photofacial

IPL stands for Intense Pulsed Light. Unlike a traditional laser, which uses a specific wavelength, IPL delivers controlled pulses of broad-spectrum light to target selected chromophores in the skin. It is commonly used for photorejuvenation and improving visible discoloration and redness.

  • Sunspots and age spots.
  • Freckles and selected pigmentation.
  • Redness and visible superficial blood vessels.
  • Rosacea-associated redness in appropriately selected patients.
  • Uneven skin tone.
  • Signs of sun damage.

An IPL photofacial is a non-invasive treatment designed to improve the appearance of sun-damaged skin and uneven complexion. The light energy is absorbed by targeted pigment or blood vessels, allowing the body to gradually clear treated areas.

Most patients describe a quick snapping sensation, similar to a rubber band against the skin. Cooling and appropriate treatment settings help improve comfort.

Many patients benefit from three to five treatments, generally spaced several weeks apart. The treatment plan depends on the amount of pigmentation, redness, skin type, and response.

Pigmented spots may temporarily darken before gradually flaking or fading. Redness and mild swelling can occur for a short period.

  • Redness and swelling.
  • Temporary darkening of pigmentation.
  • Blistering or burns.
  • Temporary or persistent pigment changes.
  • Rare scarring.

IPL is not appropriate for every skin tone or every type of pigmentation. A proper assessment is important, particularly for patients with darker skin tones or recent sun exposure.

Injectable Aesthetics

Botox and Dysport

Botox and Dysport are prescription neuromodulators that temporarily relax selected facial muscles responsible for expression lines. They work by reducing the release of acetylcholine at the neuromuscular junction, decreasing muscle contraction. The goal is to soften dynamic wrinkles while maintaining natural facial expression.

Common treatment areas include:

  • Forehead lines.
  • Frown lines between the eyebrows.
  • Crow’s feet.
  • Brow positioning.
  • Selected lower-face concerns.
  • Neck bands.

Additional areas may be considered based on anatomy, clinical judgment, and the specific product’s approved indications.

Both contain botulinum toxin type A, but they are different formulations. They differ in formulation, diffusion characteristics, dosing units, and certain clinical applications. Neither product is universally better. The choice depends on your anatomy, treatment goals, and provider assessment. Importantly, Botox and Dysport units are not interchangeable.

Most appointments take approximately 15–30 minutes.

  • Dysport: Initial results may appear within two to three days.
  • Botox: Initial results often appear within three to five days.

Full results generally develop over approximately 10–14 days, although individual responses vary.

Most patients experience results lasting approximately three to four months. Some patients metabolize the medication more quickly or maintain results longer.

Not necessarily. Our philosophy emphasizes natural-looking results. Treatment is customized to your facial anatomy, muscle activity, and desired degree of movement.

  • Bruising or swelling.
  • Temporary headache.
  • Injection-site discomfort.
  • Eyelid or brow drooping.
  • Asymmetry.
  • Unwanted muscle weakness.

Rarely, botulinum toxin effects may spread beyond the injection area, causing swallowing, speech, or breathing difficulties. Seek immediate medical care if these occur.

We generally recommend avoiding strenuous exercise immediately after treatment and following your individual post-treatment instructions. Avoid rubbing or massaging the treated areas for the period specified by your injector.

Cosmetic botulinum toxin treatment is generally deferred during pregnancy and breastfeeding because adequate safety data are lacking.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers are injectable products used to restore volume, enhance facial contours, and soften selected facial folds. Many commonly used fillers are made from hyaluronic acid (HA), a substance naturally found in the body.

Depending on the specific FDA-approved filler and its indication:

  • Lips.
  • Cheeks.
  • Chin.
  • Facial folds.
  • Selected areas of facial volume loss.
  • Facial contour enhancement.
  • Restore lost facial volume.
  • Enhance lip shape and definition.
  • Improve facial proportions.
  • Enhance cheek definition.
  • Soften selected facial folds.
  • Improve facial symmetry.
  • Create a refreshed appearance without surgery.

Our philosophy is facial harmony, not excessive volume. We prioritize conservative treatment planning and gradual enhancement to preserve your natural appearance.

Longevity varies by product, injection location, metabolism, and individual factors. Depending on the product and area treated, results may last approximately six months to two years or longer.

Most fillers contain lidocaine, and topical anesthetic or other comfort measures may be used. Patients commonly experience pressure, mild discomfort, and temporary tenderness.

  • Swelling.
  • Bruising.
  • Tenderness.
  • Redness.
  • Temporary asymmetry.
  • Small palpable areas or lumps.

Most common reactions improve over several days to weeks.

Yes. Although uncommon, accidental injection into a blood vessel can cause tissue injury, skin necrosis, vision loss or blindness, and stroke. This is a medical procedure requiring detailed anatomical knowledge and appropriate emergency preparedness. Seek immediate medical attention for unusual or severe pain, skin blanching or discoloration, vision changes, or neurological symptoms during or after treatment.

Many hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved using hyaluronidase when clinically appropriate. Not all filler materials can be dissolved, and treatment of complications may require additional interventions.

Facials and Skin Health

HydroGlow Facial

A HydroGlow Facial is a multi-step facial treatment designed to cleanse, exfoliate, extract impurities, and hydrate the skin. Depending on the platform and treatment selected, it may incorporate specialized serums, suction-based extraction, and additional skin-enhancing technologies.

  • Removes surface debris and dead skin cells.
  • Helps clear congested pores.
  • Improves skin hydration.
  • Enhances skin radiance.
  • Improves the appearance of uneven texture.
  • Provides a refreshed appearance.

Many skin types can benefit from this treatment, but the specific serums and treatment intensity should be customized. Patients with active skin infections, open wounds, severe irritation, or certain inflammatory skin conditions may need to postpone treatment.

Most patients experience minimal downtime. Temporary redness or sensitivity can occur.

Many patients choose treatments every four to six weeks, depending on their skin goals and maintenance routine.

Yes, but timing matters. Some treatments can be combined in a single visit, while others should be separated to protect the skin barrier and minimize irritation. Your provider will determine the appropriate sequence.

Traditional facials generally involve manual cleansing, exfoliation, extractions, and masks. HydroGlow Facial treatments use specialized equipment to combine cleansing, exfoliation, suction-assisted extractions, and hydration in a controlled treatment sequence.

Chemical Peels

A chemical peel uses carefully selected exfoliating solutions to remove controlled layers of damaged skin and stimulate skin renewal. Different peel formulations and strengths target different skin concerns and depths.

  • Fine lines.
  • Uneven skin tone.
  • Sun damage.
  • Acne and clogged pores.
  • Selected acne scars.
  • Hyperpigmentation.
  • Rough skin texture.
  • Dull complexion.

Chemical peels are generally classified by depth:

Superficial peels

  • Improve dullness and mild discoloration.
  • Provide gentle exfoliation.
  • Usually involve minimal downtime.

Medium-depth peels

  • Target more significant sun damage, pigmentation, and texture concerns.
  • Often require several days to two weeks of recovery.

Deep peels

  • Produce more substantial resurfacing.
  • Require significantly greater recovery and medical supervision.

The appropriate peel depends on your skin type, condition, treatment goals, and medical history.

Patients may experience temporary tingling, warmth, stinging, or burning sensations. The intensity depends on the peel formulation and depth.

Some patients benefit from a single treatment, while others require a series. Your provider will recommend a treatment schedule based on your skin condition and goals.

Body Contouring and Skin Firming

nuFIRM Radiofrequency Body Contouring

nuFIRM is a non-invasive multi-modality aesthetic treatment system designed to improve skin firmness, smooth the appearance of cellulite, and enhance body contours. The platform combines technologies including:

  • Bipolar and multipolar radiofrequency.
  • Vacuum-assisted treatment.
  • Mechanical massage and tissue manipulation.
  • Infrared and LED light technologies.
  • Specialized applicators for different treatment areas.

The goal is to improve the appearance of skin texture, firmness, and body contours without surgery.

Radiofrequency delivers controlled energy into tissue, generating heat. This controlled thermal stimulation can promote collagen remodeling and improve the appearance of skin firmness over time. Additional modalities in the nuFIRM platform support tissue manipulation and the appearance of smoother contours.

Depending on the applicator and treatment protocol:

  • Abdomen.
  • Flanks and waist.
  • Thighs.
  • Buttocks.
  • Upper arms.
  • Back.
  • Selected facial and neck areas.
  • Mild to moderate skin laxity.
  • The appearance of cellulite.
  • Uneven skin texture.
  • Loss of skin firmness following weight loss.
  • Stubborn contour concerns.
  • Areas that appear less toned.

No. nuFIRM is not a substitute for weight loss, diet, exercise, or medical weight management. It is intended to improve the appearance of targeted areas and skin firmness.

Most patients describe the sensation as warm, comfortable, and massage-like. Vacuum and mechanical applicators may create a pulling or rolling sensation. Treatment intensity is adjusted according to the area and individual tolerance.

A series is generally recommended. A typical treatment plan may involve approximately six to twelve sessions, often scheduled weekly, depending on the treatment area and individual goals. Your provider will develop a personalized plan.

Some patients notice temporary changes in skin appearance following treatment. More meaningful improvements in firmness and texture generally require a series of treatments and may continue to develop over several weeks. Results vary between individuals.

Most patients can resume normal daily activities immediately. Temporary redness, warmth, tenderness, or bruising may occur.

Treatment may be inappropriate for patients with:

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  • Implanted electronic devices such as pacemakers or defibrillators.
  • Metal implants in the treatment area.
  • Active infection, open wounds, or significant skin disease in the treatment area.
  • Certain hernias in the treatment area.
  • Uncontrolled hypertension.
  • Certain active cancers or other significant medical conditions.

Additional contraindications may apply depending on the applicator and modality. A medical screening is required before treatment.

Massage Therapy

Massage therapy is designed to promote relaxation, reduce muscular tension, and support overall wellness. Available treatment styles may include:

  • Relaxation or Swedish massage.
  • Therapeutic massage.
  • Deep-tissue massage.
  • Targeted muscle tension treatment.
  • Customized wellness massage.

Specific services depend on the therapist’s training and scope of practice.

  • Helps reduce muscle tension.
  • Promotes relaxation.
  • May temporarily relieve muscular discomfort.
  • Supports stress management.
  • May improve perceived sleep quality.
  • Encourages a sense of overall well-being.

Session lengths may vary, commonly including 60-, 90-, and 120-minute appointments.

Massage should be adjusted to your comfort level. Deep-tissue techniques may create temporary pressure or tenderness, but treatment should not cause intolerable pain.

Frequency depends on your goals, physical activity, muscular tension, and individual response. Some patients prefer occasional maintenance sessions, while others benefit from more regular appointments.

Massage may need to be postponed or modified with:

  • Fever or active infection.
  • Acute injury.
  • Blood clots or suspected thrombosis.
  • Unexplained swelling.
  • Certain bleeding disorders.
  • Recent surgery.
  • Certain skin conditions.

Patients with significant medical conditions should discuss massage suitability with their healthcare provider.

Combining Aesthetic Treatments

Yes. Many treatments complement one another when appropriately planned. For example:

  • Botox with selected facial skin rejuvenation treatments.
  • Fillers with neuromodulator treatments.
  • HydroGlow Facial treatments with selected skin maintenance procedures.
  • IPL with an individualized pigmentation and skin-rejuvenation program.
  • Erbium Glass with other collagen-stimulating treatments.
  • nuFIRM as part of a comprehensive body-firming program.

However, treatments cannot always be performed together. Some lasers, peels, injectables, and energy-based procedures require separation to minimize inflammation, bruising, pigment changes, and other complications. Your provider will establish the appropriate treatment sequence.

Every patient has different skin characteristics, facial anatomy, and aesthetic goals. We begin with a consultation to evaluate:

  • Facial anatomy and volume.
  • Pigmentation and sun damage.
  • Signs of aging.
  • Medical history.
  • Previous aesthetic treatments.
  • Desired results and recovery preferences.

We then create a personalized treatment plan.
