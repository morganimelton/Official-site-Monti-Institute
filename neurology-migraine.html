Neurology & Migraine

Comprehensive migraine care, from prevention to relief.

At Monti Institute, we provide physician-led neurological care for patients experiencing migraine, headache disorders, and complex neurological concerns. Care is individualized, evidence-based, and focused on reducing the impact symptoms have on daily life.

Migraine Management

Reduce frequency. Improve your quality of life.

Treatment is selected based on clinical evaluation, migraine history, previous therapies, symptoms, and individual needs.

A comprehensive assessment of migraine or headache symptoms, medical history, previous treatments, and clinical needs to guide an individualized care plan.

A preventive treatment for appropriate patients with chronic migraine, used to help reduce migraine frequency and severity over time.

An IV CGRP preventive therapy for appropriate patients with migraine, administered as part of a physician-guided treatment plan.

Individualized follow-up, medication management, treatment adjustment, and clinical monitoring based on how the patient responds over time.

Insurance & Access

Neurological care, covered by most insurance plans.

Many neurological and migraine services are covered by most major insurance plans. Coverage varies by plan, diagnosis, and treatment. Our team can help patients understand the next steps for their specific care.

Acute Migraine Relief

Having a migraine now? We’re here to help.

For patients experiencing an active migraine, Monti offers physician-supervised infusion options designed to address acute symptoms and provide supportive relief.

An IV treatment option for patients experiencing an acute migraine episode, with treatment selected according to symptoms and clinical needs.

Physician-supervised IV therapy using clinically appropriate medications and supportive treatments based on the patient’s presentation.

Additional medications or IV components may be used when clinically appropriate to address symptoms such as pain, nausea, inflammation, or dehydration.
