Insurance

Coverage for medical care.

Insurance is accepted for migraine and neurological care and may also apply to certain medically indicated infusion services, including migraine rescue infusions, depending on individual plan benefits.

Don't See Your Plan?

Monti Institute is actively expanding insurance participation, including additional plans in Nevada and California. Contact our office or your insurance provider to verify current coverage and individual benefits.

Referrals

Monti Institute does not generally require a medical referral for migraine services. However, a referral may be required if the patient's individual insurance plan requires one.

Insurance participation, eligibility, benefits, and coverage vary by individual plan. Patients should confirm coverage before treatment.
