Aesthetics & Injectables

Aesthetic medicine, guided by medical expertise.

Monti Institute offers physician-led aesthetic treatments performed by trained medical professionals, with care grounded in facial anatomy, clinical judgment, proportion, and the individual goals of each patient.

Aesthetic Treatments

Designed around you.

Every treatment begins with an individualized assessment of your anatomy, goals, and the result you want to achieve.

Neuromodulator treatments can soften the appearance of expression lines and are individualized according to facial anatomy, movement, and treatment goals.

Filler treatments are used selectively to restore or enhance volume, contour, proportion, and facial balance according to individual anatomy.

Injectable treatment plans may combine appropriate techniques to address the patient's individual aesthetic goals while maintaining natural proportion and balance.

Therapeutic Botox may be used for appropriate patients to help manage excessive sweating.

The Monti Approach

Beauty, backed by medicine.

01

Assess first.

Treatment begins with an individualized assessment rather than a predetermined procedure.

Treat with intention.

03
