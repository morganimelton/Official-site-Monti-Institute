Skin & Laser

Advanced skin care, backed by clinical technology.

From advanced laser treatments and IPL to facials, skin imaging, and light-based therapies, Monti Institute combines modern technology with individualized assessment to address skin health, tone, texture, and appearance.

Skin Assessment

Understand your skin first.

01

NuNAV Skin Imaging

Advanced skin imaging used to assess the skin and help inform individualized treatment planning.

02

DermaSensor Skin Lesion Scan

A handheld skin lesion scan that may support clinical evaluation when a provider determines it is appropriate.

Laser & Light Treatments

Technology selected for your skin.

IPL treatment selected for appropriate visible redness, vascular concerns, and tone irregularity after clinical assessment.

A light-based facial protocol considered for appropriate acne-prone skin and selected according to skin assessment and tolerance.

Erbium glass technology used as part of a facial treatment plan for appropriate texture and wrinkle concerns.

Erbium glass treatment selected for appropriate scar and texture concerns after individualized evaluation.

A lighter YAG resurfacing option considered for appropriate patients seeking a conservative resurfacing treatment.

A moderate YAG-based resurfacing and tightening treatment selected according to skin goals, tolerance, and clinical judgment.

A deeper YAG resurfacing option considered only after assessment of skin goals, treatment tolerance, and clinical appropriateness.

IPL photofacial treatment selected for appropriate patients after skin assessment and discussion of goals.

Skin Support

Support your skin beyond the laser.

HydroBeauty

Red Light Therapy
